03 ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Anshika.

I'm a Communication Design student at NIFT Gandhinagar, specializing in Digital Communication and UX/UI.

I like moving between research, interfaces, identities and visual storytelling. For me, design is about understanding a problem first and then finding a visual language that makes the answer feel natural.

I'm especially interested in the space where digital experiences and visual communication meet.
